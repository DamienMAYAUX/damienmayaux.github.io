I am postdoctoral researcher at the [Université Paris Dauphine](https://dauphine.psl.eu/en/).

My research lies at the intersection of behavioural economics and industrial organization, with applications to digital economics and food consumption. 

I completed my PhD in Economics at the [Paris School of Economics](https://www.parisschoolofeconomics.eu/en/about/) in September 2025. I have been Research Fellow at the [IMT School for Advanced Studies Lucca](https://www.imtlucca.it/en/homepage) from May 2024 to October 2025.

I am co-organizer of the [Micro Lunch Seminar]{https://leda.dauphine.fr/fr/seminaire-externe/seminaires-thematiques.html} of the [LEDa]{https://leda.dauphine.fr/} (with [Alessandro Ispano]{https://sites.google.com/site/alessandroispano/}), of the [Digital Regulation working group]{https://chairgovreg.fondation-dauphine.fr/en/recherche/1328} of the [Chair Governance and Regulation]{https://chairgovreg.fondation-dauphine.fr/en} (with [Lucas Eustache]{https://chairgovreg.fondation-dauphine.fr/fr/lucas-eustache}) and of the [PJEM - Paris Junior Experimentalist Meetings -]{https://sites.google.com/view/experimentmeetings/home} (with [Béla Elmshauser]{https://www.parisschoolofeconomics.eu/personnes/bela-elmshauser/})
